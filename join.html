Join Us!

Are you interested in showing off your school spirit in the peppiest group on campus? Looking for friendships to last a lifetime? Hungry for regular visits to local Mexican restaurants? Just want an easy, fun way to continue playing music in college? UCSD Pepband is the group for you!

The best way to join is to simply show up at any of our weekly rehearsals. We rehearse every week from 7:00 pm – 9:00 pm on Tuesdays at the RIMAC Concourse Lobby which is located between the gym/arena and soccer field (right next to the RIMAC ticket booths). This location does occasionally change, so be sure to sign up for our mailing list to get weekly reminders of when and where we’re playing. If you can’t make rehearsals, don’t worry! Simply come to gigs and we’ll welcome you right in.

You are welcome to join us at any point during the year. We do not require any prior musical experience, time commitment, monetary requirements, or even auditions for our members, so if you show up, you’re in!

Performance times are listed every week under the Home tab. Check back frequently for updates. You can also sign up for our mailing list through the Resources tab.

Have questions? Check out our FAQ page or email us at pepband@ucsd.edu.
